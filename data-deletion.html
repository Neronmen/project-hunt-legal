Request data deletion

Last updated: September 27, 2026
You can ask the Project Hunt owner to delete data associated with your use of Project Hunt's Facebook integration.

How to submit a request

  1. Email khoapunse183214@fpt.edu.vn with the subject “Project Hunt data deletion request.”
  2. Identify the Facebook Page by its name and Page ID, and provide the Facebook account or contact details needed to help locate the relevant integration data.
  3. Describe what you want deleted, such as stored Page connection information, access tokens, or publishing records. The owner may contact you to clarify or verify the request.
For your security, do not include your password or send an access token in your request.

What happens next

The Project Hunt owner will review the request and remove the identifiable data related to the Facebook integration that can be located and deleted from the system, subject to applicable requirements and operational backups.

You may also revoke Project Hunt's access from your Facebook settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized API calls but does not itself delete information already stored by Project Hunt.

Related policies

See the Privacy Policy for more information about data handling, retention, and your rights.
