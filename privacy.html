Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026
Project Hunt is an internal tool for managing content and publishing videos to Facebook Pages. This policy explains what information the tool processes when its Facebook integration is used.

Information processed

Depending on the requested publishing activity, Project Hunt may process a Facebook Page ID and name, a Page access token needed to call Facebook APIs, video and content metadata, captions, comments, publish status, Facebook post IDs, and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the system.

How information is used

Information is used to connect a Facebook Page, publish videos and comments requested by the user, carry out related publishing actions, record publishing status to help prevent duplicate posts, and operate or debug Project Hunt.

Facebook and Meta integration

When authorized, Project Hunt uses Facebook Login/OAuth, a Facebook Page access token, and the Facebook Graph API. Facebook and Meta process information under their own terms and policies. Users can manage or revoke the app's access through their Facebook settings.

Data retention

Information is retained in Project Hunt only as needed to support the integration, publishing history, and system operation. Retention can depend on local system configuration and backups.

Data security

Access tokens are stored locally by Project Hunt and used to make authorized API requests. The system owner is responsible for protecting the machine and files where this information is stored. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing and third-party services

Project Hunt does not sell personal data or share it with advertising providers. Information is sent to Facebook/Meta as needed to provide the requested integration and publishing actions. Other infrastructure or service providers may process technical data only when needed to operate the system.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of the Facebook integration, subject to applicable requirements and the system owner's ability to identify that information.

Data deletion

To request deletion of data related to use of the Facebook integration, contact the Project Hunt owner using the contact below. You may also revoke the app's access in Facebook settings. Revoking access does not itself remove information already stored in Project Hunt.
